Add the two-tone style: 92 solid icons on a 16 grid, a light body with accents in the brand colour, each with a hover motion that acts out what it means. Import them from a `duotone` path with the same names and props as the layered icons, so switching style is one import: `kubai-icons/duotone`, `kubai-icons-react/duotone`, `kubai-icons-vue/duotone`, and `svg-duotone/` in `kubai-icons-static`. The primary follows `color`; the light tone is the primary at 40% over white, or `--kd-b`.
